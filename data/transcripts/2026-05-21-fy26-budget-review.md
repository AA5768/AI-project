# FY26 Mid-Year Budget Review

Yuki Tanaka: Mid-year review against the FY26 plan. Owen has the numbers, then we make three or four decisions and stop.

Owen Brady: Revenue plan for FY26 is 48.2 million dollars. Through April we have recognised 17.4 million against a phased plan of 19.1, so we are 1.7 million behind, and essentially all of that is the two Helios-3 units that moved out of Q2 because of the Northgate excursion and the build slip.

Yuki Tanaka: Is that revenue lost or revenue moved?

Ryan is not in this meeting, but the Northgate production order is the answer to that question and it is not signed.

Owen Brady: On plan it is moved. It lands in Q4 if first customer ship holds in September.

Priya Raman: First customer ship is the thirtieth of September and I have no float left. I would plan on it holding and I would not be shocked if it moves four to six weeks.

Yuki Tanaka: Then we should model both. Owen, give me a downside case with Helios first customer ship in mid-November, before the board meeting.

Owen Brady: I will have the downside case by the twenty-ninth.

Owen Brady: On spend. R&D budget for FY26 is 11.4 million and we are tracking to 11.9. The overrun is three things: the Northgate excursion response at about 210,000 dollars including travel and the rework kits, the Halstead second-source qualification at 140,000 which was approved in March and not in the original plan, and the Kestrel dry-break and auto-teach work at about 95,000.

Yuki Tanaka: Half a million over on eleven point four is not a crisis but it has to come from somewhere. Priya, what is in the Helios-3 NRE line that we can move to FY27?

Priya Raman: The NRE line is 3.6 million. What is actually left in it is the Rev B controller board spin, which is 80,000 of NRE plus about 120,000 of engineering time, and the second production tooling set.

Yuki Tanaka: Does Rev B affect anything we ship this year?

Priya Raman: No. Rev B is a throughput improvement and a fix for the Brunnen encoder end-of-life. It matters for 2027 volume.

Yuki Tanaka: Then we cut the Helios-3 NRE line from 3.6 million to 3.2 million and Rev B moves to the FY27 plan.

Priya Raman: I will accept that with one condition. If the June throughput measurement comes in materially below the committed 450 wafers per hour, Rev B comes back on the table as a customer commitment issue and not a nice-to-have, and we find the money.

Yuki Tanaka: Accepted. Owen, book the Helios-3 NRE line at 3.2 million and put a note against it referencing this condition.

Owen Brady: Booking 3.2 million. I will note the condition in the budget file.

Greg Salinas: Capex. The ISO Class 5 assembly bay expansion is 2.1 million in the plan and we have spent 400,000 on design and permits. The build is quoted at eleven weeks and if we do not release it in June, it does not exist for the FY27 ramp.

Yuki Tanaka: What does it buy us?

Greg Salinas: Today I can build four EFEMs a month. The FY26 plan says seven in the second half and the FY27 plan says ten. Four is a hard ceiling and it is set by cleanroom floor area, not by people.

Owen Brady: Note that the second-half plan of seven a month is also above what the Kaneda allocation supports even with expedite, which caps around six.

Greg Salinas: Then we have two ceilings and we are arguing about the higher one.

Yuki Tanaka: Release the capex. A cleanroom takes eleven weeks and a supplier negotiation takes a phone call. I would rather have the floor and fight for the reducers.

Greg Salinas: I will release the cleanroom build order in June.

Sofia Marchetti: Headcount. We have three open engineering requisitions: a senior motion control engineer, a second contamination control engineer, and a field service engineer for APAC. Average time to fill across those is 94 days and the motion control req has been open since January.

Yuki Tanaka: Why is the motion control req not filled?

Sofia Marchetti: Because our band tops out at 165,000 and the two candidates who reached final round had offers at 190 and 205 from larger equipment companies.

Priya Raman: That req is the difference between Dana doing Rev B and Dana doing everything.

Yuki Tanaka: What does it cost to fix the band?

Sofia Marchetti: Moving the senior motion control band to 195,000 costs about 30,000 dollars annualised for that one hire, and it creates a compression problem with two existing engineers who are at 158 and 162.

Owen Brady: So the real number is closer to 70,000 once you level the two.

Yuki Tanaka: Decision: we raise the band for the senior motion control role to 195,000 and we level the two existing engineers in the same cycle. Owen, take the 70,000 from the contingency line, not from R&D.

Sofia Marchetti: I will reopen the search at the new band this week.

Nadia Haddad: One working capital item that was approved in March and needs to show up here. Eight weeks of Cerapoint ceramic blade buffer stock, 71,000 dollars of inventory.

Owen Brady: It is in inventory but it is not called out. I will break it out as a line in the working capital schedule.

Yuki Tanaka: Good. That is what I asked for in March and it is why I am asking again now.
